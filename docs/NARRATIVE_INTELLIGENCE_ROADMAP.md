# TaleForge Narrative Intelligence Roadmap

The architecture is intentionally modular.

1. Genre Intelligence — what kind of story.
2. Narrative Tradition Intelligence — broad structural conventions.
3. Trope Intelligence — reusable narrative devices.
4. Narrative Mode Intelligence — the mechanism that drives progression.
5. Genre Grammar Intelligence — repeatable structural cycles and constraints.
6. Compatibility Graph — whether selected components can coexist.
7. Hybrid Story Engine — how compatible components combine.
8. Novelty Engine — whether a combination is sufficiently differentiated.
9. Inversion Engine — deliberately transforms genre expectations.
10. Story Opportunity Engine — searches the combination space for new story opportunities.
11. Story Concept Engine — converts an opportunity into an original premise.
12. Story Architecture — DNA, identity, blueprint, ending, arcs, depth.
13. Generation Context — carries the architecture into chapter creation.
14. Quality / Continuity / Governance — prevent weak or inconsistent output.
15. Performance Intelligence — feeds real reader outcomes back into future opportunity selection.

No layer should silently replace another. Each has a distinct purpose and explicit dependencies.
