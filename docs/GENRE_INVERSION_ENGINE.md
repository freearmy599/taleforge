# TaleForge Genre Inversion Engine

The Inversion Engine transforms genre expectations into alternative structural rules.

It does not randomly make a story unusual. Each inversion records:
- the expectation being transformed
- the structural transformation
- required grammar
- compatibility requirements
- risks
- payoff opportunities

Current governed patterns include progression vulnerability, uncertain future knowledge, logistics-first military conflict, dangerous truth in mystery, choice-over-destiny relationships, alliance costs, false safety, and value-based character growth.

The engine validates an inversion against required grammar, compatibility, and sustainability before it can proceed.

Pipeline:
Hybrid Recipe -> Novelty -> Inversion -> Story Opportunity.

Inversion is not a substitute for originality validation; it is one controlled source of structural differentiation.
