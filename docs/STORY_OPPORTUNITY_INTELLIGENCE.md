# TaleForge Story Opportunity Intelligence

Story Opportunity Intelligence identifies promising narrative spaces before story concept generation.

It evaluates:
- compatibility
- novelty
- distance from the TaleForge catalog
- long-serial sustainability
- payoff potential
- inversion opportunities

The opportunity layer does not generate prose and does not modify existing series.

Decision bands:
- 80+: high priority
- 65-79.99: promising
- 50-64.99: explore more
- below 50: reject

Pipeline:
Genre Intelligence + Traditions + Modes + Tropes + Grammars
-> Compatibility
-> Hybrid Recipe
-> Novelty
-> Inversion
-> Story Opportunity
-> Story Concept
-> Story DNA

The eventual autonomous creator should consume approved opportunities rather than bypassing this governance layer.
