Privacy

Last updated October 2026

What TaleForge stores

TaleForge can store anonymous reading events such as story opens, chapter starts, reading progress, follows, bookmarks, and chapter completion. These events use an anonymous browser identifier and are used to improve discovery and reading features.

Local library

Without an account, follows, bookmarks, reading history, and reading progress are kept in your browser's local storage.

Accounts

If account features are introduced, the information required for those features will be described here before they are used.

Third-party services

TaleForge uses infrastructure services to host the site and its data. External services may process technical requests required to deliver the site.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method published by the TaleForge operator.
