The Last Train Home

Some journeys take you somewhere. Others take you back.

TaleForge Original · 8 min read

The station was almost empty when Daniel arrived.

It was well past midnight, and the old clock above Platform 4 had stopped at 12:17. Daniel checked his phone. No signal.

He looked down the platform.

The lights flickered once.

Then a train appeared.

Daniel frowned. He had lived beside this station for years, but he had never seen a train arrive this late.

The doors opened without a sound.

There was only one passenger carriage with its lights still on.

Daniel hesitated before stepping inside.

The carriage was completely empty.

He walked toward the nearest seat and noticed something written on the window.

His own name.

Daniel stepped back.

The doors closed.

The train began moving.

Outside the window, the city disappeared almost immediately. Buildings gave way to fields, then forests, and finally darkness.

Daniel tried the emergency button.

Nothing happened.

Then someone sat down across from him.

Daniel looked up.

It was an older version of himself.

The stranger smiled quietly.

"You finally came back."

Daniel stared at him.

"Back from where?"

The older man looked through the window.

"From the place you have spent your whole life trying to forget."

The train slowed.

Ahead, Daniel could see a small station surrounded by warm yellow lights.

He recognized it immediately.

It was the station from his childhood.

The place where his family had once lived.

The train stopped.

The doors opened.

Daniel stepped onto the platform.

For the first time in years, he felt as though he had finally arrived somewhere.
