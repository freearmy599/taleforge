The Last Train Home

Some journeys take you somewhere. Others take you back.

TaleForge Original · 8 min read
```
```

The station was almost empty when Daniel arrived.

It was well past midnight, and the old clock above Platform 4 had stopped at 12:17. Daniel checked his phone. No signal.

He looked down the tracks.

Nothing.

Then he heard it.

A distant rumble growing louder in the darkness.

A train slowly appeared through the mist.

Daniel frowned.

He had never seen this train before.

There were no advertisements on its sides. No destination was displayed above the doors. Every window was dark.

The train stopped directly in front of him.

The doors opened.

Inside, the carriage was completely silent.

Daniel hesitated.

Then he noticed someone sitting near the back.

An old man was looking directly at him.

"You're late," the man said.

Daniel stepped inside.

"Where does this train go?"

The old man smiled.

"Home."

The train began moving.

Outside the window, the city disappeared.

Buildings became fields. Fields became forests. Then even the moon vanished behind a wall of clouds.

Daniel tried his phone again.

This time, the screen showed a date he didn't recognize.

He looked at the old man.

"What year is it?"

The man didn't answer.

Instead, he pointed toward the window.

Daniel looked outside.

A small house stood beside the tracks.

He knew that house.

It was his childhood home.

But there was something impossible about it.

The lights were on.

And someone was waiting outside.

Daniel stood up.

"Stop the train."

The old man finally looked at him.

"You can't get off until you remember why you left."

Daniel stared at the house.

For the first time in years, he remembered the sound of his mother's voice calling him home.

The train began to slow.

The doors opened.

Daniel stepped onto the platform.

Behind him, the train disappeared into the darkness.

He walked toward the house.

And for the first time in a very long time, he wasn't afraid of going home.
